Peel Region: Income vs. Population Density

PythongeopandasstatsmodelsSpatial statistics

Does population density predict household income across Peel Region, and do the results have a geographic pattern?

  • Analyzed 272 census tracts: income is spatially clustered (Global Moran's I = 0.19), while density clusters only weakly (0.07).
  • Regressed income on density (R² = 0.08, about $2,750 lower income per 1,000 more people/km²) and showed the residuals were still clustered (Moran's I = 0.21), so spatial regression is the next step.
  • Recomputed density in a projected coordinate system after finding that Web Mercator inflates tract areas by about 1.9× at this latitude.

EPL Match Outcome Model

Pythonpandasscikit-learn

A logistic regression that predicts home win, draw, or away win from each team's recent scoring and conceding form.

  • Used four seasons of results and a time-based split: trained on 2021/22 to 2023/24, tested on 2024/25.
  • Excluded each match from its own form numbers to avoid leakage, and caught a bug that flipped away teams' goals by hand-checking features.
  • 48.0% accuracy against a 41.1% always-home-win baseline. The model never predicts draws, which the README discusses.

PythonRPublished analysis

A Substack article testing whether more intense pressing goes with fewer goals conceded.

  • Two seasons of team data across Europe's top five leagues, ten simple linear regressions in R.
  • A positive relationship in all ten, but statistical significance in only 5.
  • Limitations stated plainly: 18 to 20 teams per model, association rather than causation, multiple comparisons.
